# Research

MobiLab studies perception, navigation, interaction, and task execution on mobile robots.

<div className="academic-overview">
<section>

## Autonomous navigation and mapping

Localization, mapping, sensor fusion, and motion control for autonomous mobile robots in indoor and outdoor environments.

</section>
<section>

## Humanoid and quadruped robotics

Locomotion, manipulation, and control on humanoid and quadruped platforms, including robotic hands and operation in human environments.

</section>
<section>

## Embodied AI

Machine learning for perception, decision-making, and task execution on physical robots. Topics include reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and vision-language-action (VLA) models.

</section>
<section>

## Human-robot interaction and teleoperation

Natural interfaces and remote operation for robot supervision and task execution.

</section>
<section>

## Manipulation and simulation

Kinematic and dynamic modeling, motion planning, and integration of manipulators, grippers, and sensors using ROS 2 and MoveIt 2. Simulation in Gazebo, MuJoCo, and Isaac Lab supports testing before experiments on hardware.

</section>
</div>
