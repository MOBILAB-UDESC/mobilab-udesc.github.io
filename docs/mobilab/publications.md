# Publications

Selected publications by MobiLab researchers. Titles and bibliographic information follow the published records. This is a selection, not a complete bibliography.

## 2025

### Discovering Control Scheduler Policies Through Reinforcement Learning and Evolutionary Strategies

Aureo Guilherme Dobrikopf, Gabriel Abatti, and Douglas Wildgrube Bertol.

*Actuators*, 14(12), 604 (2025). Journal article.

The authors list MobiLab at UDESC Joinville as their affiliation. The paper investigates learned policies for selecting controllers, comparing reinforcement learning and evolutionary strategies.

[DOI and article](https://doi.org/10.3390/act14120604) · [Code](https://github.com/AureoGD/control_scheduling)

## 2024

### Adaptive Imune Fuzzy Quasi-Sliding Mode Formation Tracking Control for Wheeled Mobile Robots with Obstacle Avoidance Under Incidence of Uncertainties and Disturbances: An Artificial Immune Systems Inspired Approach

Willy John Nakamura Goto, Douglas Wildgrube Bertol, and Nardênio Almeida Martins.

*Learning and Nonlinear Models*, 22(1), 61-95 (2024). Journal article.

The paper studies formation tracking and obstacle avoidance for differential-drive mobile robots under uncertainties and disturbances.

[DOI and article](https://doi.org/10.21528/lnlm-vol22-no1-art5)
