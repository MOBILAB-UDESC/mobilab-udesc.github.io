Learn the AI and simulation tools that control real robots, from the researchers who use them every day

The Summer School presents the tools and methods MobiLab uses every day to train students in robotics and AI. The course covers the full cycle of a project: building the hardware, simulating, integrating with ROS 2, teleoperating, collecting data and training an AI model.

The format is hands-on. Each theory block is followed by hands-on practice, and the week ends with a challenge in which the teams run their models on the robots.

ABOUT MOBILAB RESEARCH LAB

The Autonomous Systems and Mobile Robotics Laboratory of the Center of Technological Sciences (CCT) at UDESC.

Founded in 2023 by Professor Douglas Bertol, in cooperation with the Dynamic Legged Systems (DLS) lab at the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia.

A fully equipped lab with humanoid and quadruped robots, including the Unitree G1, H1, B2, B2W, Go1 and Go2 research platforms.

Integration

Data

DAY 5 Fri · Feb 19

Learning

  • AITraining models (ACT, VLA, WLA) on the collected dataset and evaluating them
